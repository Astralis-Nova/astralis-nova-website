Rules of Engagement

Standard Mode

Traditional legal chess with castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate and draw detection powered by chess.js.

Nova Tri-Deck

All pieces begin on the Core deck. Pieces move normally on a deck. Non-pawns may spend a turn phase-shifting to the same square on an adjacent deck. Pawns may phase-shift only while standing on a glowing portal node. Capture the opposing High Commander to win.

Async Play

Create a match, share the game code or invite link, then close the page. Each move is saved in D1. The portal checks for an opponent move automatically while open.

Piece Identities

King: High Commander. Queen: Oracle Core. Rook: Citadel. Bishop: Phase Seer. Knight: Warp Strider. Pawn: Drone Sentinel.
