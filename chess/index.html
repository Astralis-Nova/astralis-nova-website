Rules of Engagement

Standard Mode

Traditional legal chess with castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate and draw detection powered by chess.js.

Nova Tri-Deck

Three staggered 4×4 command decks and four playable 2×2 attack platforms form one 64-square battlefield. Units move normally on their current board. Any unit may transfer between an attack platform and its four linked deck cells. Non-pawns may phase-shift to the same empty square on an adjacent command deck; Drone Sentinels use the four glowing portal cells. Capture the opposing High Commander to win.

Async Play

Create a match, share the game code or invite link, then close the page. Each move is saved in D1. The portal checks for an opponent move automatically while open.

Character Pieces

King: High Commander. Queen: Oracle Core. Rook: Citadel Droid. Bishop: Phase Seer. Knight: Warp Strider. Pawn: Drone Sentinel. Silver fields explorers and mystics; Void fields armored warlords and machines.
