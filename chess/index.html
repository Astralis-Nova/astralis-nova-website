RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Chess chooses the battle. You choose the survivor.

1 · Maneuver

Each campaign opens with a randomized legal formation; all movement and destinations follow standard chess.

2 · Intercept

A capture pauses the board before either unit is removed.

3 · Duel

Move your ship and fire energy bolts. Nova controls the opposing unit.

4 · Resolve

The winner survives on the board; the loser is removed. Then the next turn begins.
