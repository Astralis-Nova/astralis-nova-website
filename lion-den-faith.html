Daniel 6 · Courage Under Pressure

Daniel and the Lions’ Den

A story of integrity, prayer, courage, and protection when faith is tested in the darkness.

The Story

Daniel served faithfully in the kingdom, and his wisdom earned the trust of the king. Other officials became jealous and searched for a reason to accuse him, but they could find no corruption or dishonesty in his life.

They persuaded the king to issue a law forbidding prayer to anyone except the king for thirty days. Daniel knew about the law, yet he continued his regular practice of praying to God. His enemies saw him and reported him.

The king was deeply troubled, but the law could not be changed. Daniel was placed in the lions’ den, and a stone was set over its entrance. The king spent the night without food or entertainment, unable to sleep.

At dawn, the king hurried to the den and called out to Daniel. Daniel answered that God had sent an angel to shut the lions’ mouths because he had been found innocent. Daniel was lifted from the den without injury.

The heart of the story Courage is not the absence of danger. It is the decision to remain faithful when fear, pressure, and uncertainty surround us.
