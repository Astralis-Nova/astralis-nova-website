Just A River

Author Unknown

Preserved from Ramon Bivens’ original Angelfire page. The wording and spelling below remain as they appeared in the archived poem.

A river flows through an through

Building a world of its own.

The beds forms slowly

with grains of earth.

The longer it exists

The more unique it becomes.

Some see a river as just a river.

I see it as a sort of love.
