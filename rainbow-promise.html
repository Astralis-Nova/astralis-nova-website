A THOUGHT BORN AFTER THE RAIN

The Rainbow
Promise

One rainy day, the clouds opened and a rainbow appeared. It brought to mind the story of Noah, the flood, and a promise placed across the sky for every generation to remember.

After the Flood

In the book of Genesis, Noah and his family stepped from the ark after the floodwaters receded. God established a covenant with Noah, his descendants, and every living creature.

The rainbow became the sign of that covenant: the earth would never again be destroyed by a flood of that kind. It is a symbol of mercy, remembrance, and hope after judgment and hardship.

Why a Rainbow Matters

A rainbow forms when sunlight enters raindrops, bends, reflects, and separates into many colors. Nature supplies the physics, while faith gives the sight a deeper layer of meaning.

After darkness, rain, and thunder, color appears. The storm is not the final word. The rainbow becomes a bridge between what has passed and what may still be possible.

“I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.”
Genesis 9:13, King James Version
🌧️🌈

A Reflection From This Orbit

When the rain passes and a rainbow rises, pause for a moment. Remember that storms can leave marks without owning the future. Remember the ark, the covenant, and the ancient promise carried in the colors above us.

The rainbow does not pretend the storm never happened. It appears because the rain and the light meet.

May every rainbow be a quiet reminder that hope can arrive while the clouds are still moving away.✦ ASTRALIS NOVA
