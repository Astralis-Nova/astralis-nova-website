# Rainbow Promise bird photographs

- `dove-flight-photo.webp`: Karen Arnold, “Dove Flying in Sky”, free public-domain photo: https://www.publicdomainpictures.net/en/view-image.php?image=256495&picture=dove-flying-in-sky
- `raven-flight-photo.webp`: NPS / Emily Hassell, Joshua Tree National Park, “Raven flying (Corvus corax)”, January 24, 2021. Public domain, work of a US National Park Service employee: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raven_flying_(Corvus_corax)_(51192501139).jpg

These are cutouts edited from the downloaded photographs with the built-in image editing tool, rather than newly generated bird images. The original sky backgrounds were removed for transparent compositing. SVG masks separate the photographic wing sections from the body for animated wingbeats, gliding and banking. The dove's near wing is reflected to form a balanced pair because the oblique original photograph foreshortens its rear wing; the photographic body and tail are retained.

Editing prompt: “Extract only the exact photographed bird into a transparent PNG. Remove all sky, including between feathers. Preserve the original pose, anatomy, orientation, colors and photographed feather detail. No redraw, new pose, added objects, glow, shadow, text or background.”

Original photographs remain available through the source links above. On-page credits link to both photographers' sources.
