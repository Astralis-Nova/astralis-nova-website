The Father's House

A place to imagine welcome, love, and being together again.

An artistic reflection inspired by Scripture. The mystery leaves room for wonder.

John 14:2–3 · King James Version

“In my Father's house are many mansions … I go to prepare a place for you.”

Jesus offers his disciples reassurance as they face separation. We imagine those many dwelling places as a welcome, held in the hope of being with him.

For those we carry in our hearts.
