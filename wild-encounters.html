Why this world exists

The desert keeps introducing itself.

This is not meant to be a polished wildlife encyclopedia. It is a growing record of actual encounters. When something crawls, flies, slithers, wanders, or unexpectedly appears, the story can land here.

Each entry can mix the original photo or video with the story of the encounter, verified creature facts, practical safety information, and a little commentary from the human who happened to be standing there.

Field rule: Respect wildlife, keep a safe distance, and let the real animal be more interesting than the camera shot.

📓 What each encounter can include

  • Original photo or video
  • Date and general location
  • The real story of what happened
  • Creature identification
  • Interesting facts and behavior
  • Safety notes when appropriate
  • Field commentary and humor
